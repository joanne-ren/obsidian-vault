## This Meeting 2026-10-07

### Topic 1: Trust as premise and as asset
- Shoppers who believe the default is fair don't watch prices while shopping.
- Two sides: for the shopper, trust is saved effort; for the retailer, trust is an asset, un-vigilant shoppers pay the regular price.

### Topic 2: Measurement logic
- Scanner data can't see trust, only the behavior trust governs = how much attention shoppers spend on price.
- So the DV is price vigilance; trust is the theoretical explanation behind it.
- Writing rule: say "consistent with a trust mechanism", not "proves trust declined".

### Topic 3: Loyalty ≠ vigilance
- Store loyalty (share of wallet, trip frequency, churn) and price vigilance are distinct outcome families.
- A shopper can keep coming because the store is close, while at the same time watching prices more. Keep the two separate.

### Topic 4: Channels through which ESL may change behavior
- Signal: shopper thinks "they can change prices anytime now" → more vigilant, even if actual prices didn't move.
- Actual pricing: re-pricing is now costless → store really re-prices more often / runs more promos → shopper is just responding to real change.
- Easier comparison: labels carry unit price, promo, QR code → comparison because its cost dropped, not because of distrust.
- Accuracy (reverse): shelf price = checkout price → fewer overcharges at checkout → trust may rise.

### Topic 5: Channel × store positioning (moderators)
- Signal channel: stronger at EDLP stores, their whole proposition is "stable, trustworthy prices".
- Actual-pricing channel: stronger at Hi-Lo stores, easier for them to add promotions.
- Comparison channel: mostly a category thing, stronger where unit price is hard to compare; little to do with store positioning.

### Topic 6: A more general vigilance measure
- Elasticity / stockpiling are near-useless for must-buy staples (milk, bread): quantity doesn't move.
- More general: gap of each purchase price from the lowest price available at the time (gap-to-lowest-available-price).
- More vigilant shoppers buy more often at the low point → the gap shrinks.
- Doesn't rely on being able to stockpile or on promotions existing; covers all categories.

## Decisions
- DV = price vigilance; trust stays as mechanism/interpretation, not a claim.
- Keep loyalty and vigilance as separate outcome families.
- Adopt gap-to-lowest-available-price as the all-category vigilance measure.
