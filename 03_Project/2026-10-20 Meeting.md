## This Meeting 2026-10-20

### Topic 1: ESL

**Event**
- First ESL adoption; share of stores covered.

**What retailers do**
- Repricing frequency.
- Repricing magnitude: more small changes?
- Promotion frequency, depth, and duration.
- Speed of matching competitors' price changes.
- Effect on fresh / near-expiry items: waste and sales.
- Efficiency

**How customers react**
- Spend share at the chain; whether they switch to other stores.
- Trip count and basket size per trip.
- Buying more at low prices; stockpiling.
- Private-label share.
- Change in price sensitivity.
- Heterogeneity across households: age, income, regulars vs occasional shoppers.

**Impact on the retailer**
- Sales.
- Labor cost.
- Loyalty program.



**Discussed during the meeting**
- Uses annual data: within the same year, pilot-vs-rollout timing matters little. 
- For cross-year cases, run sensitivity analysis (include/exclude) or control for rollout duration.

**Database design** 
principle: collect as much as possible first, filter later

- Multiple date columns: announcement date, first-pilot date, majority-of-stores rollout date, planned/announced completion date.
- One free-text notes column for the full timeline.
- Source column.

**Data sources**
- Factiva, Wharton WRDS, company info. Target real rollouts, not announcements.
- Platform reports also worth checking (e.g. Flywheel ESL reports).
- Export all Flywheel retailers, filter later.

**Explanatory variables** 
- Retailer: EDLP vs Hi-Lo dummies etc., from Flywheel or self-built.
- Country: link external international databases
- ESL itself: supplier
- Practice: keep a two-column sheet, left = variables that might matter, right = expected direction
