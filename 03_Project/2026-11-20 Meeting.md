### Pilot of LLM Product Recommendations

We are interested in which brands an AI assistant recommends when a consumer uses it to look for a product. 

Four design questions guide it:
- (i) **Country.** The API already passes the consumer's location as a parameter. Does stating the country in the question text ("I'm in the UK, …") still change the recommendation?
- (ii) **Wording.** Does the wording of an otherwise identical question change the recommendation?
- (iii) **Time of day.** Does the hour at which the question is asked matter?
- (iv) **Cost.** What would sustained daily collection cost?

**Design.**

| Dimension | Values | n |
|---|---|---|
| Product | anti-dandruff shampoo; stain-removing laundry detergent | 2 |
| Wording | "Which … should I get?"; "What's a good …?"; "Can you recommend …?" | 3 |
| Country in question text | stated; not stated | 2 |
| Time slot (local) | 9–11h; 15–17h; 20–22h | 3 |
| Market | UK, US, Germany, France (each asked in its local language) | 4 |

This yields 12 replies per market per slot, and 144 replies per day. Setup details:
- **Model.** We query `chat-latest`, OpenAI's alias for the Instant model currently serving ChatGPT.
- **Search and location.** Web search is forced on every call. The country and time zone are always passed as the consumer's location.
- **Stateless calls.** Each call is independent (`store=False`) and carries no memory or context.
- **What we log.** Every raw response, together with its sources, token usage and local collection time.
- **Data collected.** Between 13 and 17 November 2026 the pilot gathered 657 valid replies. Collection ended when the API credit was exhausted.

Two features of this setup deserve candour:
- **API ≠ app.** The setup appears, at first glance, to reproduce what a new ChatGPT user would see. Yet the API omits the app's system prompt and its shopping interface, so what we measure is the recommendation at the model layer rather than the screen a consumer sees.
- **Unversioned model.** `chat-latest` returns no version identifier, so a model update can only be inferred indirectly, for instance from answer length and token use. Both have so far remained stable.

**Early observations.** The analysis is available in the [pilot notebook](https://github.com/joanne-ren/ai-shopping-pilot/blob/main/analysis/pilot_analysis.ipynb). Unless stated otherwise, figures refer to the **mention rate**, the share of replies that name a brand.

- **A small brand pool.** Recommendations are highly concentrated:
  - About 80 replies per market and product name only 5 to 12 distinct brands, and each reply names 2 to 3 of them.
  - Every market has between one and three lead brands with a mention rate close to 100%:
    - UK: Nizoral, Ariel and Persil
    - US: Tide, Head & Shoulders, Selsun and Nizoral
    - Germany: Ketozolin and dm's own label, Denkmit
    - France: Ducray and Ariel
  - The model may thus compress a category into a short, recurring list, one that changes only at the margins.

- **Country in the question text.** Stating the country appears redundant, since the API already supplies it. Yet in Germany and France it can reorder the mid-tier brands considerably:
  - In French detergent, Persil's mention rate falls from 73% to 19% and Skip's rises from 20% to 60%.
  - In the UK and US the effect is small.
  - The sources hint at a mechanism. Once the country is named, the model's searches tend to name it as well, and the cited pages tilt toward local retailers and media.

- **Wording.** Wording matters about as much as stating the country, and in the same place:
  - Lead brands hold steady.
  - Mid-tier brands move. In French shampoo, Vichy is named in 75%, 61% and 21% of replies across the three wordings.
  - The same question, phrased slightly differently, can thus surface one brand and underweight another.

- **Time of day.** No consistent difference emerges across the three slots. They can reasonably be treated as repeated draws.

- **Cost.** Each reply costs about $0.06. A design of 48 replies per day would cost roughly $88 a month and $535 over six months.
