### Topic 2: ESL

The gap
- No large-sample evidence on what actually happens in real grocery stores.
	- ESL lowers the cost of changing prices, so retailers should reprice more often, run more promotions, and match competitors faster.
	- Consumer concern:Fear of "Uber-style surge pricing on demand."

What retailers say vs do
- Retailers claim they don't do dynamic pricing; what they mainly do is markdowns on near-expiry items.

Finding data
- Look for cases with a clear, concentrated go-live time: short rollout period, with a completion date.
