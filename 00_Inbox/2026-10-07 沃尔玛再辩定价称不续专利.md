# 沃尔玛再辩定价：称不会续专利

来源：Retail Dive（记者 Dani James），首发于 Retail Dive，Grocery Dive 转载，2026-10-07。延续 [[2026-10-05 沃尔玛否认个性化定价]] 一周前 CEO Furner 的公开表态，这次是沃尔玛更正面的回应。

- **事件**：沃尔玛企业事务执行副总裁 Dan Bartlett 周一致信消费者权益组织 Groundwork Collaborative 的总裁兼 CEO Lindsay Owens，要求对方「更正记录（correct the record）」，称其公开言论「反复歪曲」沃尔玛的定价实践与技术用途。这是沃尔玛迄今最直接的回应。
  - Walmart EVP of corporate affairs Dan Bartlett sent a Monday letter to Groundwork Collaborative's president and CEO Lindsay Owens, asking her to "correct the record," saying her public statements "repeatedly mischaracterize" Walmart's pricing practices and use of technology. It's Walmart's most direct response yet.

- **沃尔玛立场**：Bartlett 重申，沃尔玛不会、也将来不会把电子货架标签（digital shelf labels）用于个人化或动态定价；并称「专利不等于计划」，专利只描述一项发明与潜在能力，不代表已部署、已对顾客使用、或已决定未来使用。
  - Bartlett reiterated Walmart does not and will not use digital shelf labels for individual or dynamic pricing; a patent "describes an invention and potential capabilities," which does not mean Walmart has deployed, used it with customers, or decided to.

- **关键新进展：不续专利**：针对 2023 年获批的货架标签远程控制专利（其中写明可依据扫码购物车内容动态调价），沃尔玛发言人称「专利已授权，但我们不会使用、也不会续展（will not use it or renew it）」。该专利调整后的到期日为 2039 年 4 月。
  - Key new development: on the 2023 shelf-label remote-control patent (which states prices can change dynamically based on scan-and-go cart contents), a Walmart spokesperson said "the patent has issued, but we will not use it or renew it." Its adjusted expiration is April 2039.

- **Groundwork 反驳**：Owens 称「定价混乱是沃尔玛自己造成的」，指沃尔玛对顾客、对投资人、对美国专利商标局（USPTO）各说一套。她与 Elizabeth Pancotti 的周一报告梳理了沃尔玛多项定价技术专利，并论证「企业不会花钱雇专才去研发并申请专利，只为把它锁进硬盘永不上线」。
  - Owens rebutted that "the confusion is of Walmart's own making," saying Walmart tells customers one thing, investors another, and the USPTO something else. Her Monday report with Elizabeth Pancotti catalogs several Walmart pricing-tech patents, arguing firms don't develop and patent tech just to shelve it unused.

- **监管背景**：个性化/动态定价过去一年引发监管关注，FTC 已介入个性化定价（见 [[2026-10-05 FTC就个性化定价征求意见]]），马里兰州成为全美首个「禁止基于监控数据瞬时抬价的价格操纵做法」的州。
  - Regulatory backdrop: the FTC has taken up personalized pricing, and Maryland became the first state to ban "price manipulation driven by instantly spiking prices based on surveillance data."

- **低价心智的张力**：卡内基梅隆 Heinz 学院数字媒体与营销教授 Ari Lightman 指出，沃尔玛长期以「天天低价（everyday low pricing, EDLP）」著称，这与动态定价的逻辑并不相容。
  - EDLP tension: Carnegie Mellon Heinz professor Ari Lightman noted Walmart's long-standing "everyday low pricing" reputation doesn't lend itself to dynamic pricing.

- **为何值得记**：把「专利能力 vs 公开否认」的张力推到了新节点，沃尔玛首次用「不续专利」这种可验证动作来自证清白，但对手反将一军，质问「为何花钱申请却声称不用」。看点有二：一是企业「个性化但不碰价格」的边界如何用具体承诺来兑现，二是品牌心智（EDLP）本身成为否认动态定价的信誉资产，这把价格公平/信任的讨论与品牌定位绑在了一起。
  - Worth noting: it advances the "patented capability vs public denial" tension to a new node, with Walmart offering a verifiable act (not renewing the patent) to prove its case, countered by "why patent it if you won't use it." Two angles: how a "personalize but don't touch price" boundary gets cashed out in concrete commitments, and how brand positioning (EDLP) itself becomes a credibility asset for denying dynamic pricing, binding price-fairness/trust to brand identity.
