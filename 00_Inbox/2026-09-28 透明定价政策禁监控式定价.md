# 透明定价政策：禁止用个人数据定价

来源：与上条同为 Groceryshop 2026 综述报道中提及；原文未点名具体城市，只称「市议会」周二批准。[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/groceryshop-2026-artificial-intelligence/831386/)

- **事件**：某市议会（原文未点名城市）周二批准「全面透明定价政策（comprehensive transparency pricing policy）」。
  - A city council (city unnamed in the source) approved a "comprehensive transparency pricing policy" on Tuesday.

- **禁止项**：禁止大型实体店和线上零售商使用性别、种族、浏览记录、社交媒体记录等信息来确定商品价格，即禁止基于个人数据的监控式/个性化定价（surveillance / personalized pricing）。
  - It bans large brick-and-mortar and online retailers from using data such as gender, race, browsing history and social media to set prices, i.e. surveillance/personalized pricing.

- **要求项**：商店须向所有顾客清晰张贴商品价格，并为老年人等群体提供传统优惠券和「透明折扣（transparent discounts）」。
  - Stores must post prices clearly for all customers, and provide traditional coupons and "transparent discounts" for groups such as seniors.

- **为何值得记**：直接关涉个性化/算法定价、价格歧视与隐私的合法边界，是「数据用于定价」这一 AI 用例踩线时的政策一手材料。呼应 EuroCommerce 立场里「无偏见/无歧视、GDPR 合规」作为信任维度的论述，也把折扣从「个性化投放」重新拉回「对所有人透明」的价格公平议题。
  - Worth noting: it goes to the legal limits of personalized/algorithmic pricing, discrimination and privacy, a first-hand policy artifact for the "data-for-pricing" AI use case when it crosses the line. It echoes EuroCommerce's framing of no-bias/no-discrimination and GDPR compliance as trust dimensions, and pulls discounts back from personalized targeting toward price fairness that is transparent to everyone.
