# Groceryshop 2026：AI 热情高，数据地基未跟上

来源：Grocery Dive（Groceryshop 2026 综述，拉斯维加斯），2026 年 9 月。[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/groceryshop-2026-artificial-intelligence/831386/)

- **总体立场**：Instacart 与克罗格（Kroger）高管都看好 AI 抹平线上线下购物界限的潜力，但同时警告，若企业缺乏高质量、干净的数据就仓促部署 AI，反而可能引入错误和不确定性。热情很高，基础还没跟上。
  - Both Instacart and Kroger executives were bullish on AI erasing the online/offline line, but warned that deploying AI on poor, unclean data can introduce errors and uncertainty. Enthusiasm is high; the foundation is not yet there.

- **陷阱（Cossett）**：克罗格首席数字官科塞特（Yael Cossett）指出，一个潜在陷阱是许多零售商缺乏有效开展 AI 购物所需的高质量数据，而消费者行为始终是个棘手问题。
  - Cossett flags a pitfall: many retailers lack the high-quality data needed to do AI shopping well, and consumer behavior remains a hard problem.

- **谁主导客户体验尚不明朗**：AI 购物普及仍处起步阶段，目前不清楚谁会主导客户体验，因为大型 AI 公司、Instacart 等电商平台、以及零售商自身都在推出 AI 购物工具（三方争夺）。
  - It is unclear who will own the customer experience: big AI companies, e-commerce platforms like Instacart, and retailers themselves are all launching AI shopping tools (a three-way contest).

- **零售商的两难**：杂货商虽卖商品、握有食品专业知识，但正如过去几年电商蓬勃发展时一样，他们正在探索一项并非其核心业务的技术。
  - The bind: grocers sell goods and hold food expertise, yet, as with the e-commerce boom of recent years, they are exploring a technology outside their core business.

- **为何值得记**：与 Schnucks「数据整合是苦活」、克罗格「AI 是整合信号的前提」互为印证，把「数据就绪度（data readiness）」确立为跨零售商的共同瓶颈。「谁拥有客户」这一问题呼应 EuroCommerce/McKinsey agentic commerce 里「交易被平台截获 vs 导回品牌」之争。
  - Worth noting: it echoes Schnucks ("data integration is grunt work") and Kroger ("AI is the prerequisite for consuming signals"), establishing data readiness as a shared cross-retailer bottleneck. The "who owns the customer" question maps onto the agentic-commerce tension between platform capture and redirect-to-brand.
