# Groceryshop 2026

来源：Coresight Research 对大会第一、二、三日的回顾，以及 Grocery Dive 的综述报道。本笔记按主题（而非按日）整理。

---

## AI assistants: understanding intent, completing missions, expanding discovery（意图、任务与发现）

- **最常被提及的用例：「晚饭吃什么？」**。零售商越来越把 AI 助手看作一种能理解购物意图（shopping intent）的方案，从而减轻购物时的认知负担（cognitive load），无需逐件添加商品。
  - The most-cited use case is "what's for dinner?". Retailers see AI assistants as a way to grasp shopping intent and cut cognitive load, without adding items one by one.

- **为什么杂货尤其适合 AI（Instacart CEO 克里斯·罗杰斯 Chris Rogers）**：该品类兼具高频与高复杂，消费者要反复权衡偏好、饮食需求、预算、促销和家庭需求。无需逐件搜索，只需表达更宽泛的需求（如「给我一份孩子爱吃的健康餐，40 美元以内」），AI 即可生成相关方案。
  - Grocery suits AI because it is both high-frequency and high-complexity: shoppers repeatedly weigh preferences, dietary needs, budget, promotions and household needs. Instead of item-by-item search, they state a broad need ("a healthy meal my kids will like, under $40") and AI generates a solution.

- **从搜 SKU 到解决问题（DoorDash）**：消费者越来越想要解决问题的帮助，而非搜索具体商品编号（SKU）。举例：策划儿童生日派对、找齐某道餐的食材、管理饮食限制、在 SNAP（补充营养援助计划）资格等限制条件下购物。
  - DoorDash: consumers increasingly want help solving problems, not searching SKUs. Examples: planning a kids' birthday party, gathering ingredients for a meal, managing dietary restrictions, shopping under constraints like SNAP eligibility.

- **AI 扩大发现范围（关键发现）**：使用 AI 的顾客订购的独特 SKU（unique SKU）数量增加 60%，说明 AI 可以扩大商品发现范围，而不仅是加速重复购买。
  - Customers using AI ordered 60% more unique SKUs, showing AI can broaden product discovery, not just speed up repeat purchases.

- **简化流程不等于减少购物量（克罗格 Kroger）**：经验显示简化购物流程不一定减少购物车商品数。其 AI 助手利用照片、手写食谱和个性化信号来生成购物篮。
  - Kroger: simplifying the journey doesn't necessarily shrink the cart. Its assistant builds baskets from photos, handwritten recipes and personalized signals.

## From pilots to deployment: results and tools（从试点到落地的成效与工具）

- **总趋势**：AI 购物助手正从试点（pilot）走向真实落地（deployment）。
  - AI shopping assistants are moving from pilots to real deployments.

- **Ask DoorDash 与逐步走高的指标**：DoorDash 近期推出对话式的 Ask DoorDash 助手（可上传食谱创意、生成购物车）。方（Fang）分享：AI 功能上线之初购物车规模增长 35%、结账速度提高三倍；后来分别提升至购物车规模 +50%、结账速度 +5 倍。
  - DoorDash launched the conversational Ask DoorDash assistant (upload recipe ideas, generate carts). Per Fang: at launch cart size +35% and checkout 3× faster; later improving to +50% cart size and 5× faster checkout.
  - 背景：DoorDash 是美国按需配送平台，外卖起家，如今大举进入生鲜杂货配送市场。

- **Migros 内容提效**：瑞士零售商 Migros 用 AI 把营销内容制作从几天压缩到几分钟。
  - Swiss retailer Migros compressed marketing-content creation from days to minutes using AI.

- **Albert Heijn（荷兰 Ahold 旗下）**：最初推出一款主打膳食灵感的 AI 助手，解决「晚餐吃什么」，体验建立在 2 万多道菜谱的数据库上。随顾客接受度提高，再把助手扩展到客流量更高的购物环节。
  - Albert Heijn first launched a meal-inspiration assistant for "what's for dinner," built on 20,000+ recipes, then expanded it into higher-traffic parts of the journey as acceptance grew.

- **下一阶段：从建议转向行动**。未来功能包括个性化膳食计划、预装购物篮（pre-loaded baskets）、对话式偏好更新，以及优惠券激活、更改门店选择等服务型任务。
  - Next phase: from suggestion to action, with personalized meal plans, pre-loaded baskets, conversational preference updates, and service tasks like coupon activation or changing store selection.

## Personalization: from segments to contextual 1:1（个性化：从客群细分到情境化一对一）

- **总体方向**：AI 应精准满足每位顾客的个人需求，而非笼统的客群划分（segment）；重点是从传统细分走向更具情境性的一对一体验。多数公司仍处在「从细分到真正 1:1」的过渡期。
  - AI should target individual needs, not broad segments: moving beyond segmentation toward contextual, one-to-one experiences. Most companies are still mid-transition from segment-based to true 1:1.

- **illy（Cristian Arcangeli，市场营销、战略及数字业务副总裁）**：目前企业更多是「细分」而非「个性化」，即便客群切得更细，也不等于真正了解单个客户；illy 约 80% 的工作仍基于客户细分。
  - illy's Cristian Arcangeli: companies still do more segmentation than personalization; finer segments are not the same as truly knowing the individual, and about 80% of illy's work is still segment-based.

- **Albertsons（Harshad Agashe，数据科学与 AI 产品副总裁）**：个性化应以客户的实际需求为中心，不仅要知道买了什么，更要知道他们想达成的目标，例如在预算约束下为特定场合搭配合适的商品。
  - Albertsons' Harshad Agashe: personalization should center on real needs, knowing not just what customers bought but what they want to accomplish, e.g. assembling the right items for an occasion within a budget.

- **iFood（Vinicius Oliveira，技术总监）**：更进一步，用基于交易和生态系统数据训练的自研大型电商模型，按每位顾客及其情境定制体验；模型不只是推荐产品，而是按情境调整整个购物体验（如理解工作日午餐与周末晚餐的需求不同）。
  - iFood's Vinicius Oliveira: a proprietary large e-commerce model trained on transaction and ecosystem data, tailoring the experience per customer and context, not just recommending products but adjusting the whole experience by context (weekday lunch vs weekend dinner).

## Redefining value beyond price（价值被重新定义：不止价格）

- **「价值」的维度扩宽**：价值（value）如今涵盖自有品牌品质（private-label quality）、健康定位（health positioning）与会员权益（membership perks），不再只看价格。
  - "Value" now spans private-label quality, health positioning and membership perks, not just price.

- **「例外消费（exception spending）」（科利达 Circana）**：每一笔消费都受到更严格审视。为应对价格压力，购物者会换品牌（被提及最多）、转向自有品牌、更换零售商，或干脆少买。但价格并非唯一考量，消费者仍优先投入新鲜农产品、健康、营养和宠物相关支出。
  - Circana's "exception spending": every purchase gets more scrutiny. To cope with price pressure shoppers switch brands (most-cited), move to private label, switch retailers, or buy fewer items. Yet price isn't the only factor: they still prioritize fresh produce, health, nutrition and pet spending.

- **自有品牌是结构性转变**：自有品牌（private label）不再只是更便宜的替代品，正日益成为在健康、营养和信任上的差异化来源。在消费者和 AI 代理都能更容易比价的环境下，独家产品与差异化组合会越来越重要。
  - Private label is a structural shift: no longer just a cheaper alternative, increasingly a source of differentiation on health, nutrition and trust. As consumers and AI agents compare prices more easily, exclusive products and differentiated assortments matter more.

## Foundations: data and execution — "technology is just the first step"（数据与执行是地基）

- **数据就绪度是前提**：Instacart 与克罗格高管都看好 AI 抹平线上线下界限的潜力，但警告：缺乏高质量、干净的数据就仓促部署 AI，可能引入错误和不确定性。热情很高，基础还没跟上。克罗格首席数字官科塞特（Yael Cossett）指出，许多零售商缺乏所需的高质量数据，而消费者行为始终棘手。
  - Both Instacart and Kroger execs are bullish on AI erasing the online/offline line but warn that deploying on unclean data introduces errors and uncertainty. Cossett notes many retailers lack the high-quality data needed, and consumer behavior remains hard.

- **品牌 vs 零售商的数据资产互补**：品牌懂产品与定位，零售商懂顾客购买行为与购物篮构成；整合两者需要准确的数据集成和清晰的流程。
  - Brands know products and positioning, retailers know purchase behavior and basket composition; combining these requires accurate data integration and clear processes.

- **仅靠技术不够（Slalom 市场推广负责人 Jason P.）**：「技术只是第一步」，信任、治理（governance）和清晰的用例才是智能体应用成功落地的关键。
  - Technology alone isn't enough (Slalom's Jason P.): "technology is just the first step," with trust, governance and clear use cases decisive for agentic deployment.

## Who owns the customer? Agentic commerce and loyalty（谁拥有客户？代理式商务与忠诚度）

- **谁主导客户体验尚不明朗（三方争夺）**：大型 AI 公司、Instacart 等电商平台、以及零售商自身都在推出 AI 购物工具。杂货商虽卖商品、握有食品专业知识，但正如过去几年电商蓬勃发展时一样，他们在探索一项并非其核心业务的技术。
  - A three-way contest over the customer experience: big AI companies, platforms like Instacart, and retailers themselves are all launching AI tools. Grocers sell goods and hold food expertise, yet, as with the recent e-commerce boom, they are exploring a technology outside their core business.

- **代理式商务的关系风险（agentic commerce）**：当 AI 购物助手开始替消费者做购买决策（挑选、比价甚至下单），零售商可能失去几十年建立起来的直接客户关系。
  - As AI agents start making purchase decisions (choosing, comparing, ordering), retailers risk losing the direct relationship they spent decades building.
  - 背景：agentic commerce 指 AI 不只推荐商品，而是直接代表消费者完成决策，零售商与消费者之间因此多了一层「AI 代理」。

- **对忠诚度的挑战（埃森哲 Accenture 全球研究主管兼董事总经理 Karen Fang Grant）**：AI 助手能减轻决策疲劳（decision fatigue），但也可能冲击传统忠诚度模式。若 AI 代理能跨品牌、跨零售商比价，品牌就需要更强的差异化，而不能只靠消费者的习惯或熟悉度。
  - Accenture's Karen Fang Grant: assistants can ease decision fatigue but may challenge traditional loyalty models. If agents compare across brands and retailers, brands need stronger differentiation, not just habit or familiarity.

## My notes / research connections（观点与研究连接）

- **发现扩大 vs 曝光集中**：DoorDash「独特 SKU +60%」用量化数据佐证了克罗格「产品发现率上升」的说法，支持「对话式界面可能比信息流/搜索式推荐更能扩散曝光」这一假设。但同一套推荐系统也可能把注意力集中到少数被算法青睐的商品上，值得追问其曝光分布与可审计性（auditability）。
  - Discovery vs concentration: DoorDash's +60% unique SKUs backs Kroger's "more discovery," supporting the hypothesis that conversational interfaces spread exposure more than feed/search. Yet the same rec-sys can concentrate attention on a few favored items, so exposure distribution and auditability are worth probing.

- **「谁拥有客户」= 截获 vs 导回品牌**：三方争夺与代理式关系风险，正对应 EuroCommerce/McKinsey 里「交易被平台截获 vs 导回品牌」之争，也是 co-branding 与信任中介的核心命题。
  - "Who owns the customer" maps onto the platform-capture vs redirect-to-brand tension (EuroCommerce/McKinsey), and to the co-branding and trust-intermediary questions.

- **价值≠仅价格 = 折扣议题的另一面**：竞争维度从价格扩到品质、健康与会员，自有品牌从「更便宜」转为「差异化」，与反身性零售/低价零售的隐忧互为镜像。
  - Value beyond price is the flip side of the discount debate: competition widens from price to quality, health and membership, and private label shifts from cheaper to differentiated, mirroring reflexive-retail / low-price concerns.

- **数据就绪度是跨零售商的共同瓶颈**：与 Schnucks「数据整合是苦活」、克罗格「AI 是整合信号的前提」互为印证，说明「热情」与「地基」之间存在系统性落差。
  - Data readiness is a shared bottleneck across retailers, echoing Schnucks ("integration is grunt work") and Kroger ("AI is the prerequisite for consuming signals"), a systematic gap between enthusiasm and foundation.
