# Groceryshop 2026（拉斯维加斯）

来源：Coresight Research 对大会第一、二、三日的回顾。Coresight 是专注零售与消费行业的独立研究机构，长期跟踪门店开关与行业趋势数据。相关的 Grocery Dive 综述另见 [[2026-09-28 Groceryshop2026 AI热情vs数据地基]]。

## 第一日：AI 理解购物意图，价值超越价格（Day One）

### 「晚饭吃什么」成为头号用例

- **最常被提及的消费者用例**：解决反复出现的家庭难题「晚饭吃什么？」。零售商越来越把 AI 助手看作一种能理解购物意图（shopping intent）的方案，从而减轻购物时的认知负担（cognitive load），无需逐件添加商品。
  - The most-cited consumer use case is the recurring household problem "what's for dinner?". Retailers increasingly see AI assistants as a way to understand shopping intent and cut cognitive load, without adding items one by one.

- **为什么杂货尤其适合 AI（Instacart CEO 克里斯·罗杰斯 Chris Rogers）**：该品类兼具高频与高复杂，消费者要反复权衡偏好、饮食需求、预算、促销和家庭需求。无需逐件搜索，只需表达更宽泛的需求（如「给我一份孩子爱吃的健康餐，40 美元以内」），AI 即可生成相关方案。
  - Grocery suits AI because it is both high-frequency and high-complexity: shoppers repeatedly weigh preferences, dietary needs, budget, promotions and household needs. Instead of item-by-item search, they state a broader need ("a healthy meal my kids will like, under $40") and AI generates a solution.

### 各零售商实践

- **克罗格（Kroger）**：经验显示简化购物流程不一定减少购物车商品数。其 AI 助手利用照片、手写食谱和个性化信号来生成购物篮。
  - Kroger's experience: simplifying the journey doesn't necessarily shrink the cart. Its AI assistant builds baskets from photos, handwritten recipes and personalized signals.

- **Albert Heijn（荷兰 Ahold 旗下）**：最初推出一款主打膳食灵感的 AI 助手，解决「晚餐吃什么」，体验建立在 2 万多道菜谱的数据库上。随顾客接受度提高，再把助手扩展到客流量更高的购物环节。
  - Albert Heijn first launched a meal-inspiration assistant for "what's for dinner," built on a database of 20,000+ recipes, then expanded it into higher-traffic parts of the journey as acceptance grew.

- **下一阶段：从建议转向行动**。未来功能包括个性化膳食计划、预装购物篮（pre-loaded baskets）、对话式偏好更新，以及优惠券激活、更改门店选择等服务型任务。
  - Next phase: from suggestion to action, with personalized meal plans, pre-loaded baskets, conversational preference updates, and service tasks like coupon activation or changing store selection.

### 数据资产整合与「技术只是第一步」

- **品牌 vs 零售商的数据资产互补**：品牌懂产品与定位，零售商懂顾客购买行为与购物篮构成；整合两者需要准确的数据集成和清晰的流程。
  - Brands know products and positioning, retailers know purchase behavior and basket composition; combining these assets requires accurate data integration and clear processes.

- **仅靠技术不够（Slalom 市场推广负责人 Jason P.）**：「技术只是第一步」，信任、治理（governance）和清晰的用例才是智能体应用成功落地的关键。
  - Technology alone isn't enough: "technology is just the first step," with trust, governance and clear use cases being decisive for successful agentic deployment.

### 价值重构与「例外消费」

- **价值在跨品牌、渠道、形式整合**：为应对价格压力，杂货购物者采取一系列行动，换品牌（被提及最多）、转向自有品牌、更换零售商，以及干脆少买。
  - Value is consolidating across brands, channels and formats: to cope with price pressure, shoppers switch brands (the most-cited action), move to private label, switch retailers, or simply buy fewer items.

- **「例外消费（exception spending）」（科利达 Circana）**：每一笔消费都受到更严格审视。但价格并非唯一考量，消费者仍优先投入新鲜农产品、健康、营养和宠物相关支出。
  - Circana calls it "exception spending": every purchase gets more scrutiny. Yet price isn't the only factor, shoppers still prioritize fresh produce, health, nutrition and pet spending.

- **自有品牌是结构性转变**：自有品牌（private label）不再只是更便宜的替代品，正日益成为在健康、营养和信任上的差异化来源。
  - Private label is a structural shift: no longer just a cheaper alternative, increasingly a source of differentiation on health, nutrition and trust.

- **AI 对忠诚度的挑战（埃森哲 Accenture 全球研究主管兼董事总经理 Karen Fang Grant）**：AI 助手能减轻决策疲劳（decision fatigue），但也可能冲击传统客户忠诚度模式。若 AI 代理能跨品牌、跨零售商比价，品牌就需要更强的差异化，而不能只靠消费者的习惯或熟悉度。
  - AI assistants can ease decision fatigue but may challenge traditional loyalty models: if agents compare options across brands and retailers, brands need stronger differentiation, not just habit or familiarity.

## 第二日：AI 从试点走向落地（Day Two）

- **总趋势**：AI 购物助手正从试点（pilot）走向真实落地（deployment）。
  - AI shopping assistants are moving from pilots to real deployments.

- **DoorDash 量化战果**：其 AI 助手让客单价（average cart size）提升 35%-50%、结账速度提升五倍，因为它帮顾客一次性完成「购物任务（mission）」，而非逐个搜索单品。
  - DoorDash says its AI assistant lifted average cart sizes 35–50% and cut checkout time fivefold by helping shoppers complete a "mission" rather than search for single items.
  - 背景：DoorDash 是美国按需配送平台，外卖起家，如今大举进入生鲜杂货配送市场。

- **Migros 内容提效**：瑞士零售商 Migros 用 AI 把营销内容制作从几天压缩到几分钟。
  - Swiss retailer Migros compressed marketing-content creation from days to minutes using AI.

- **为何值得记**：给「AI 助手有用」提供了具体量化证据（客单价↑、结账时间↓）。「基于任务（mission-based）」购物 vs 逐个单品搜索，是购物者体验与零售摩擦角度的关键机制。
  - Worth noting: hard, quantified evidence that assistants pay off. "Mission-based" shopping vs single-item search is the key mechanism from a shopper-experience and retail-friction angle.

## 第三日：重定义价值与代理式商务（Day Three）

- **个性化下沉到个人**：AI 应精准满足每位顾客的个人需求，而非笼统的客群划分（segment）。
  - AI should target individual needs, not broad segments.

- **价值被重新定义**：「价值（value）」如今涵盖自有品牌品质（private-label quality）、健康定位（health positioning）与会员权益（membership perks），不再只看价格。
  - "Value" now spans private-label quality, health positioning and membership perks, not just price.

- **最尖锐挑战：代理式商务（agentic commerce）**：当 AI 购物助手开始替消费者做购买决策（挑选、比价甚至下单），零售商可能失去几十年建立起来的直接客户关系。
  - The sharpest challenge: as AI agents start making purchase decisions for consumers (choosing, comparing, ordering), retailers risk losing the direct relationship they spent decades building.
  - 背景：agentic commerce 指 AI 不只推荐商品，而是直接代表消费者完成决策，零售商与消费者之间因此多了一层「AI 代理」。

- **为何值得记**：代理式关系风险呼应 EuroCommerce/McKinsey 的「交易被平台截获 vs 导回品牌」之争与「谁拥有客户」命题。「价值≠仅价格」则是折扣/低价议题的另一面：竞争维度从价格扩到品质、健康与会员。
  - Worth noting: the agent-relationship risk maps onto the "platform capture vs redirect-to-brand" tension and the "who owns the customer" question. "Value beyond price" is the flip side of the discount/low-price theme: competition widens from price to quality, health and membership.
