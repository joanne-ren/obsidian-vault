# 荷兰跨境购物：糖税或推高价格套利

来源：Distrifood，2026-09-23。[原文链接](https://www.distrifood.nl/201161/jaarlijks-e21-miljard-aan-boodschappen-over-de-grens-suikertaks-stuwt-dat)

- **事件**：荷兰消费者每年在比利时和德国购买食品杂货的支出估计达 21 亿欧元。此为荷兰食品工业联合会（FNLI）委托 EFMI 商学院所做研究的估算。
  - Dutch consumers are estimated to spend about €2.1 billion a year on groceries in Belgium and Germany, per a study by EFMI Business School commissioned by the Dutch food industry federation FNLI.

- **机制**：研究人员预计，荷兰拟议中的「糖税」（对含糖饮料/食品加税）将进一步放大这种跨境购物，因为消费者会为避开本国更高的含糖商品价格而选择到邻国采购。
  - Researchers expect the proposed Dutch "sugar tax" (a levy on sugary drinks/foods) to push cross-border shopping further, as consumers head to neighboring countries to dodge higher domestic prices on sugary items.

- **为何值得记**：税收政策在国境两侧制造价格差，把「在哪买」变成一道价格套利题；对研究价格敏感度、渠道选择与折扣定位相关，也提示单国政策的效果会被邻国价格「漏损」稀释。
  - Worth noting: tax policy opens a cross-border price gap, turning "where to buy" into price arbitrage. Relevant to price sensitivity, channel choice and discount positioning, and a reminder that a single-country policy leaks across borders.
