# Plus：促销更频，却未必更便宜

- **事件**：Distrifood 一项调查显示，荷兰连锁超市 Plus 在 CEO 阿特·范哈伦（Aart van Haren）带领下大幅增加促销，尤其知名品牌的"超级折扣"明显增多。
  - A Distrifood survey finds Dutch chain Plus, under CEO Aart van Haren, has sharply ramped up promotions—especially "super discounts" on well-known brands.

- **关键褶皱**：促销**数量**上升的同时，促销商品的**平均价格**也随之走高——促销更频繁了，但整体未必更便宜。
  - The wrinkle: as the *number* of promotions rose, the *average price* of promoted items rose too—more frequent promotions, but not necessarily cheaper overall.

- **为何值得记**：这是"折扣悖论/反身性零售"在一家传统超市里的小型印证——高频促销更像一台注意力/engagement 机器，而非真正的降价；折扣的"信号"与折扣的"实惠"被拆开了。
  - Worth noting: a small confirmation of the discount paradox / reflexive retail in a mainstream grocer—high-frequency promotion behaves more like an attention/engagement engine than a real price cut; the *signal* of a discount is decoupled from its *substance*.
