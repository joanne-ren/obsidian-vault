# Costco 把部分"曝光逻辑"让渡给平台

- **发生了什么**：Costco 开始通过 Uber Eats、DoorDash 的 App 接配送订单，商品从此落入平台自己的搜索排序与推荐算法，而非 Costco 自己的货架/卖场陈列逻辑。
  - Costco now routes delivery orders through Uber Eats' and DoorDash's apps, so its products sit inside those platforms' search & recommendation logic rather than Costco's own merchandising.

- **为什么重要**：这是「[[平台渠道权力]]」这一张力较干净（虽程度有限）的印证——顾客能否"看到"商品，越来越取决于第三方的排序算法，而非零售商自己的货架位置或门店设计。关联 [[零售摩擦]]：技术让渡的正是零售商对曝光的掌控。
  - A clean (if modest) confirmation of platform/algorithmic channel power: shopper exposure is increasingly mediated by a third party's ranking algorithm, not the retailer's shelf placement or store design.

- **关键褶皱**：Costco 不是走投无路被迫上平台的弱势方，而是会员粘性极强的强势企业**主动**做此取舍，换取增量触达与配送速度；其核心的仓储会员渠道（仍完全掌控体验）大概率仍是主要顾客关系。
  - The wrinkle: Costco isn't a weak retailer forced onto platforms — it's dominant and high-loyalty, deliberately trading control for reach and speed, while its core warehouse-membership channel (full control) presumably stays primary.

- **更准确的判断**：与其说"平台正在侵蚀零售商权力"，不如说这是零售商主动运营的**双轨制**——核心渠道完全掌控，辅助渠道用部分掌控权换速度与触达。
  - Better framing: not "platforms erode retailer power" but a deliberate dual-track system — full control in the core channel, partial control (traded for speed/reach) in the auxiliary one.

- **开放问题**：即便像 Costco 这样高粘性的零售商，长期通过平台中介下单，会不会逐渐让忠诚感从"Costco 会员体系"迁移到"配送 App 的便利体验"？（关联 [[购物者体验]]）
  - Open question: even for a retailer as sticky as Costco, does repeated platform-mediated ordering gradually shift loyalty from Costco's membership to the delivery app's convenience?
