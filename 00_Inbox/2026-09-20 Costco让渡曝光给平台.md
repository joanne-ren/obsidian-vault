# Costco 把部分"曝光逻辑"让渡给平台

- **发生了什么**：Costco 开始通过 Uber Eats、DoorDash 的 App 接配送订单，商品从此落入平台自己的搜索排序与推荐算法，而非 Costco 自己的货架/卖场陈列逻辑。
  - Costco now routes delivery orders through Uber Eats' and DoorDash's apps, so its products sit inside those platforms' search & recommendation logic rather than Costco's own merchandising.

- **为什么重要**：这是「[[平台渠道权力]]」这一张力较干净（虽程度有限）的印证——顾客能否"看到"商品，越来越取决于第三方的排序算法，而非零售商自己的货架位置或门店设计。关联 [[零售摩擦]]：技术让渡的正是零售商对曝光的掌控。
  - A clean (if modest) confirmation of platform/algorithmic channel power: shopper exposure is increasingly mediated by a third party's ranking algorithm, not the retailer's shelf placement or store design.

- **关键褶皱**：Costco 不是走投无路被迫上平台的弱势方，而是会员粘性极强的强势企业**主动**做此取舍，换取增量触达与配送速度；其核心的仓储会员渠道（仍完全掌控体验）大概率仍是主要顾客关系。
  - The wrinkle: Costco isn't a weak retailer forced onto platforms — it's dominant and high-loyalty, deliberately trading control for reach and speed, while its core warehouse-membership channel (full control) presumably stays primary.

- **更准确的判断**：与其说"平台正在侵蚀零售商权力"，不如说这是零售商主动运营的**双轨制**——核心渠道完全掌控，辅助渠道用部分掌控权换速度与触达。
  - Better framing: not "platforms erode retailer power" but a deliberate dual-track system — full control in the core channel, partial control (traded for speed/reach) in the auxiliary one.

- **开放问题**：即便像 Costco 这样高粘性的零售商，长期通过平台中介下单，会不会逐渐让忠诚感从"Costco 会员体系"迁移到"配送 App 的便利体验"？（关联 [[购物者体验]]）
  - Open question: even for a retailer as sticky as Costco, does repeated platform-mediated ordering gradually shift loyalty from Costco's membership to the delivery app's convenience?

## 补充（2026-09-22）：事实更新 + 从「让渡」到「多平台对冲」

- **事实更新**：48 小时内，Costco 在 DoorDash（约 630 家门店、覆盖 47 州）与 Uber Eats（约 600 家门店）同步上线全国当日达配送，叠加在 2017 年起就合作的 Instacart 之上；CEO Ron Vachris 称当日达业务增速已超过公司整体数字业务。
  - Within 48 hours Costco launched nationwide same-day delivery on DoorDash (~630 stores, 47 states) and Uber Eats (~600 stores), on top of Instacart (partnered since 2017); CEO Vachris says same-day is now growing faster than Costco's overall digital business.

- **为何契合渠道权力**：Costco 的品牌 identity 恰恰建立在"自己掌控被看见"——多年坚持"寻宝式（treasure-hunt）"门店陈列、长期不做自营电商配送。如今同一批商品经三个独立配送 App 触达顾客，等于三套各自独立的搜索排序与广告位逻辑共同决定"顾客最先看到什么"，而这套逻辑 Costco 并不能完全掌控。
  - Costco's identity rests on controlling exposure via treasure-hunt merchandising and long avoiding its own e-commerce delivery; now three independent delivery apps' ranking/ad logics jointly decide what shoppers see first.

- **复杂化（不是"平台完全俘获零售商"）**：若属完全俘获，Costco 应只依赖单一强势平台；但它同时押注三家，本身就是一种**多平台对冲（multi-homing / hedging）**——对任何一家平台而言 Costco 都"可被替代"，因而保留了议价空间。
  - Not full platform capture: betting on three platforms at once is multi-homing/hedging—Costco is substitutable to any single one, preserving bargaining room.

- **三平台都无法复制的资产**：付费会员关系（行业续费率普遍 >90%）——相当一部分顾客忠诚仍系于 Costco 自身品牌，而非平台算法。
  - An asset no platform can replicate: paid membership (renewal rates >90%), so much loyalty stays tied to Costco's brand, not the platform's algorithm.

- **可验证的研究问题**：零售商同时接入多个配送平台，究竟**稀释**议价能力（没有哪个平台非它不可，顾客总能在别处找到它），还是**增强**议价能力（平台之间为争夺更有利展示位而互相竞争，如 Uber 提供的额外激励）？可比较同一零售商在"单平台 vs 多平台"安排下的**搜索排名与广告位占比**来验证。
  - Testable question: does multi-homing *dilute* a retailer's bargaining power (it's replaceable everywhere) or *strengthen* it (platforms compete for better placement)? Test by comparing the retailer's search ranking & ad-slot share under single- vs multi-platform arrangements.
