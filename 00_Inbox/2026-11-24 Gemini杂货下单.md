# 门店还是顾客选的，品牌可能不再是（Gemini 杂货下单）

## 新闻钩子
- Google 把 Gemini 的「连接应用（Connected Apps）」扩展到杂货：用户可以点名 Target、Safeway 等零售商，在 Gemini 里直接组好购物篮并下单。
  - Google extended Gemini's Connected Apps to grocery: users can name a retailer such as Target or Safeway and have the basket built and ordered inside Gemini.
- 据报道，在此之前 Instacart 是唯一的杂货类 Connected App。
  - Reportedly, Instacart had been the only grocery Connected App before this.

## 角度
- 考虑集（consideration set）在顾客看到货架、促销单（flyer）或赞助位（sponsored listing）之前就已形成。
  - The consideration set now forms before the shopper sees a shelf, a flyer or a sponsored listing.
- 杂货购物篮高度习惯化，自有品牌（private label）的胜出部分依赖店内可见度；当选品发生在 agent 里，这份可见度优势不再自动成立。
  - Grocery baskets are habitual, and private label wins partly through in-store visibility; once selection happens inside the agent, that visibility advantage no longer comes for free.
- 关键问题一：agent 默认选哪个，全国性品牌（national brand）、门店自有品牌，还是上周买过的那件？
  - Open question one: which item does the agent default to: the national brand, the store brand, or last week's purchase?
- 关键问题二：当「搜索」发生在零售商的围墙之外，零售媒体（retail media）的赞助位还卖给谁？
  - Open question two: what happens to retail media when the "search" happens outside the retailer's walls?
- 结论句：门店仍由顾客选择，品牌选择则越来越可能交给 agent。对自有品牌和零售媒体来说，这是结构性变化。
  - Punchline: store choice still belongs to the shopper; brand choice increasingly may not. For private label and retail media this is a structural shift.

## 英文草稿（原文）
> Last week Google opened Gemini's Connected Apps to grocery: name a retailer like Target or Safeway, and the basket gets built inside Gemini. The underappreciated part: the consideration set now forms before the shopper sees a shelf, a flyer or a sponsored listing. In grocery, where baskets are habitual and private label wins partly through in-store visibility, that matters. Which item does the agent default to: the national brand, the store brand, or last week's purchase? And what happens to retail media when the "search" happens outside the retailer's walls? Store choice still belongs to the shopper. Brand choice increasingly may not.

## 可接的研究线
- 「agent 默认选全国性品牌还是自有品牌」可以直接用 AI 购物数据集测：在提问里点名零售商，记录推荐里自有品牌的占比。
  - "Does the agent default to the national brand or the store brand" is testable with the AI-shopping dataset: name a retailer in the prompt and record the share of private-label items in the recommendations.
