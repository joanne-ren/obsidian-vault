# Jumbo：价格战里，全品类新店仍然爆满

- **事件**：荷兰艾莫伊登（IJmuiden）新开的一家 Jumbo 超市开业当天人潮涌动，经营该店的加盟商夫妇难掩激动——收银台前的长队一路排到店铺后方。
  - A newly opened Jumbo in IJmuiden (Netherlands) drew crowds on day one; the franchisee couple could barely contain their excitement, with checkout queues stretching to the back of the store.

- **为何具代表性**：即便荷兰超市**价格战**激烈，消费者对"全品类新门店"的热情依然不减——说明门店本身（新鲜感、全品类、寻宝/到场体验）仍是独立于价格之外的吸引力来源。
  - Why it's representative: even amid a fierce Dutch grocery price war, enthusiasm for a full-range *new store* holds up—suggesting the store itself (novelty, full assortment, treasure-hunt/in-person experience) is a draw independent of price.

- **可对照**：与 [[2026-09-22 Plus促销更频却未必更便宜|Plus 靠高频促销争夺注意力]] 形成对比——一条是"用折扣信号拉客"，一条是"用实体门店与全品类拉客"，两种争夺注意力的路径。
  - Contrast with Plus's promotion-driven pull: discount-signal vs physical-store/full-assortment—two routes to competing for attention.
