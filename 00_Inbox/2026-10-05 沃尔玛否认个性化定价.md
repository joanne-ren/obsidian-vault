# 沃尔玛 CEO 否认使用个性化/动态定价

来源：Retail Dive 等报道；沃尔玛 CEO John Furner（唐·傅纳）周五公开信。消费者权益组织 Groundwork Collaborative 翻出沃尔玛 2023 年专利引发质疑。

- **事件**：沃尔玛 CEO John Furner 公开否认公司用电子货架标签（electronic shelf labels）或 AI 助手 Sparky 来做个性化或基于供需的动态定价（personalized / supply-and-demand dynamic pricing）。
  - Walmart CEO John Furner publicly denied the company uses electronic shelf labels or its AI assistant Sparky to run personalized or supply-and-demand dynamic pricing.

- **CEO 原话**：不会按「你是谁」或「何时购物」定不同价格，现在不会、将来也不会；收入、购物记录、购买紧迫程度、以及「我们认为你能付多少」都不会影响价格。
  - His words: prices won't vary by who you are or when you shop, now or in future; income, purchase history, urgency, and "how much we think you can pay" won't affect price.

- **争议起点**：Groundwork Collaborative 指出沃尔玛 2023 年拿到一项电子货架标签远程控制专利，专利写明标签与价格可动态变化，例如依据购物车已有商品调价。
  - The dispute: Groundwork flagged a 2023 Walmart patent for remote control of shelf labels, which states labels and prices can change dynamically, e.g. by what's already in the cart.

- **专利举例**：「如果顾客买了金枪鱼，他们看到的蛋黄酱价格可能不同。」沃尔玛未回应 Retail Dive 关于「为何为该能力申请专利却声称不使用」的提问。
  - Patent example: "if a customer buys tuna, they might get a different price on mayonnaise." Walmart didn't answer Retail Dive on why it patented a capability it claims not to use.

- **Sparky 数据（反差点）**：8 月财报电话会上 Furner 力推 Sparky 的销售拉动，用户数同比 +70%，用 Sparky 的顾客每单消费比不用的高 40%。
  - Sparky metrics (the tension): on the August earnings call Furner touted Sparky's sales lift, users up 70% YoY, and Sparky users spending 40% more per order than non-users.

- **为何值得记**：是「数据用于定价」议题的企业一手表态，与监管端的禁令正好互为镜像，专利能力与公开否认之间的张力是核心看点。同时暴露了 AI 助手（Sparky）既被用来拉高客单价、又被撇清与定价的关系，这种「个性化但不碰价格」的边界划定值得追踪。
  - Worth noting: a first-hand corporate stance on "data-for-pricing," mirroring the regulatory bans; the gap between a patented capability and a public denial is the crux. It also shows the AI assistant (Sparky) credited with higher basket size yet distanced from pricing, a "personalize but don't touch price" boundary worth tracking.
