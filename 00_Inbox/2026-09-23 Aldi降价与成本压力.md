# Aldi 降价：成本上行期里的真降价

来源：Grocery Dive，2026-09-23。[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/aldi-price-cuts-grocery-food-inflation/831099/)

- **事件**：美国折扣杂货商 Aldi（奥乐齐）下调近三分之一日常商品价格，为顾客合计节省约 8600 万美元，活动持续至 11 月 3 日，覆盖生鲜、肉蛋白（protein）和家居用品等大类。
  - Aldi cut prices on nearly a third of its everyday range, about $86 million in combined customer savings, running through Nov 3, spanning produce, protein and household items.

- **成本背景**：降价正值零售商投入成本（input costs）普遍上升之际，其中柴油价格突破每加仑 6 美元，直接推高了把商品运往门店的运输成本。
  - The cut comes as grocers face rising input costs, including diesel above $6 a gallon, which raises the cost of trucking goods to stores.

- **为何值得记**：与 Plus「促销更频却未必更便宜」形成对照，这是成本上行期里一次真实、跨大类的降价（折扣的**实惠**，而非仅仅折扣的**信号**）。折扣定位（discount positioning）的零售商选择用规模化真降价去争夺价格敏感客群，把成本压力转成价格进攻。
  - Worth noting: a counterpoint to the Plus "more promotions, not cheaper" case. This is a real, cross-category cut during a cost squeeze (the *substance* of a discount, not just its *signal*). A discount-positioned retailer turns cost pressure into a price offensive to win price-sensitive shoppers.
