# 克罗格 × 谷歌 Gemini：个人购物助手（NRF 2026）

来源：Grocery Dive，*Kroger taps Google Gemini, unveils more key AI moves*，2026-01-13（NRF 大会，纽约）。发言人：亚埃尔·科塞特（Yael Cossett），克罗格首席数字和技术官。[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/kroger-ai-google-gemini-shopping-assistant-technology-associate-platform-sage-nrf-2026/809435/)

- **事件**：克罗格宣布扩大与谷歌云的合作，用 Gemini Enterprise 的客户体验方案搭建「新的个人购物助手（personal shopping assistant）」，建立在其不断增长的 AI 能力之上。
  - Kroger expanded its Google Cloud partnership, using Gemini Enterprise's customer-experience solution to build a "new personal shopping assistant," on top of its growing AI capabilities.

- **助手功能**：帮购物者做食品杂货计划、引导找到相关优惠和省钱方法、方便快速安排送货。
  - The assistant helps shoppers plan groceries, points them to relevant deals and savings, and makes it easy to schedule fast delivery.

- **选择悖论（Cossett 的核心洞察）**：给消费者越多选择、产品和配送选项（当天取货等），他们反而越难做决定；新助手正是切入这个「选择过载（choice overload）」痛点。
  - The choice paradox: the more choices, products and fulfillment options (like same-day pickup) Kroger gives consumers, the harder it becomes for them to decide. The new assistant is aimed precisely at this choice-overload pain point.

- **智能体集成（agentic integration）**：单一指令即可完成复杂任务；能把顾客需求转成「含可购买食材清单的指导性食谱」，并基于克罗格专有数据、实时商品种类、价格与库存给出推荐。
  - Agentic integration lets the assistant complete complex tasks from a single instruction: turning a request into a guided recipe with a buyable ingredient list, with recommendations grounded in Kroger's proprietary data and real-time assortment, price and inventory.

- **CX Agent Studio**：部署谷歌「客户体验代理工作室（Customer Experience Agent Studio）」，分析顾客致电门店的互动与意图，主动识别并更快解决问题，同时提升员工效率。
  - Kroger is deploying Google's Customer Experience Agent Studio to analyze interactions and intent when customers call stores, to proactively spot and resolve issues faster while improving associate efficiency.

- **员工侧 Sage**：克罗格在 NRF 另一场讨论中谈到继续改进员工使用的 AI 虚拟助手 Sage，即 AI 既服务顾客也服务门店员工。
  - On the employee side, Kroger discussed continuing to improve Sage, the AI virtual assistant used by associates: AI serving both customers and store workers.

- **为何值得记**：把 9 月笔记里抽象的「与前沿模型和伙伴合作」具体落到「谷歌 Gemini」这个名字，印证克罗格「自有专有数据 + 借力前沿模型」的策略（用专有数据喂 Gemini）。「选择过载」是一个新的消费者行为切口：AI 助手被定位为化解自家造成的选择摩擦。
  - Worth noting: it puts a concrete name (Google Gemini) on the abstract "partner with frontier models" from the Sept note, confirming Kroger's "proprietary data + leverage frontier models" strategy. Choice overload is a fresh consumer-behavior angle: the assistant is framed as resolving a friction the retailer itself created.
