# ChopMore：成本加 10% 的透明定价门店

来源：Grocery Dive，[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/chopmore-price-chopper-grocery-supermarkets-value/873028/)

- **事件**：堪萨斯城一家独立超市运营商把一家门店改造为「ChopMore!」，所有商品按进货成本加固定 10% 加价销售，主打「完全价格透明（full price transparency）」。
  - A Kansas City grocer converted a store into "ChopMore!," pricing every item at cost plus a flat 10% markup for full price transparency.

- **押注**：以诚信定价而非规模优势，来对抗全国连锁与折扣超市，赌独立区域超市能靠「激进的定价诚实」取胜。
  - A bet that independent regional grocers can out-compete national chains and discounters on radical pricing honesty rather than scale.

- **为何值得记**：是监控/个性化定价的镜像反面，把透明从「监管禁令、被动合规」变成「主动的品牌定位与竞争武器」。可与透明定价政策那条对读：一边是立法强制透明，一边是零售商自愿把透明做成卖点，共同指向价格公平/信任作为差异化资产。
  - Worth noting: the mirror image of surveillance/personalized pricing, turning transparency from a regulatory ban and passive compliance into an active brand position and competitive weapon. Read against the transparency-pricing policy: one side legislates transparency, the other voluntarily sells it, both pointing to price fairness/trust as a differentiating asset.
