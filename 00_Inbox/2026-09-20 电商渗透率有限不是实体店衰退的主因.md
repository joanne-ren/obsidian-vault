# 全渠道颠覆逻辑与零售商的应对策略

## 电商渗透率有限，不是实体店衰退的主因

- 全球电商渗透率约六分之一，远未达到主导地位。
  - Global e-commerce penetration is only about one-sixth of retail — far from dominant.
- 对零售商而言，电商长期是高投入、低回报的成本中心，本身并非颠覆性力量。
  - For retailers, e-commerce has long been a high-investment, low-return cost center — not a disruptive force in itself.

## 真正的颠覆来自全渠道模式（omnichannel）

- 线上的核心优势是「无限货架（endless aisle）」：SKU 不受物理空间限制。
  - Online's core edge is the endless aisle: SKU count isn't capped by physical space.
- 线下的核心优势是「即时性」：购买后立即拿到商品。
  - Offline's core edge is immediacy: you get the goods the moment you buy.
- 两种模式各有缺陷，而消费者同时想要「选择丰富度」和「获取速度」。
  - Each model is incomplete, yet shoppers want both assortment breadth and speed of access at once.
- 结果：零售商被迫把门店改造成双重职能——既是销售场所，又是履约配送中心。
  - The upshot: retailers must remake stores into a dual role — point of sale *and* fulfillment/distribution node.

## 全渠道模式代价高昂

- 消费者体验升级的同时，运营成本大幅上升。
  - The shopper experience improves, but operating costs rise sharply alongside it.
- 高成本环节集中在拣货、最后一公里配送、退货处理。
  - The cost-heavy links are picking, last-mile delivery, and returns handling.

## 成本压力向上游传导

- 零售商利润率本就微薄，又叠加履约成本上升、工资涨幅超过销售增长。
  - Retail margins are already thin, now compounded by rising fulfillment costs and wage growth outpacing sales growth.
- 维持利润的主要途径转向对品牌方（brands）施压。
  - The main way to defend margin shifts toward squeezing the brands.
- 两种手段：一是收取费用（上架费 slotting fee、促销费、零售媒体广告费 retail media），二是直接替代（自有品牌 private label）。
  - Two levers: charge fees (slotting, promotion, retail-media ad fees), or replace outright (private label).

## 渠道方角色的双重性

- 中间平台同时是渠道方，又是竞争者。
  - The intermediary is simultaneously a channel and a competitor.
- 品牌方每拓展一条新触达路径，往往同时引入一个新的利益博弈方。
  - Every new path to reach a shopper tends to introduce a new party bargaining over the spoils.
- 渠道数量增加 ≠ 品牌方控制权增加。
  - More channels does not equal more control for the brand.
