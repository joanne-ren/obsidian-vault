# 平替与自有品牌模仿：Mondelēz 诉 Aldi（dupes / private-label imitation）

自有品牌「做平替」与品牌方反击的一个法律化实例。正家理论见 [[Gielens 2025 -  copyconomy]]。与 [[2026-09-28 寻宝式轮换与新鲜感频率]] 共享 Aldi / 低价 / 自有品牌背景，但机制不同（模仿与象征价值侵蚀，而非新鲜感速率）。

- **什么是 dupe（平替）**：长得像、功能类似，但便宜很多。在超市里最典型的就是自有品牌模仿大牌。
  - A dupe looks alike and works similarly but costs far less; in grocery the classic case is private label imitating a leading brand.

- **案例：Mondelēz 起诉 Aldi**。理由是 Aldi 的自有品牌饼干包装（颜色、字体、图案）太像 Oreo、Wheat Thins，会让顾客误以为是同一家。Aldi 之后大幅改了自有品牌包装，统一标上「an Aldi original」。
  - Mondelēz sued Aldi, arguing its private-label cookie packaging (colors, fonts, patterns) looked too much like Oreo and Wheat Thins and could confuse shoppers. Aldi then substantially redesigned its private-label packaging, labeling it "an Aldi original."

- **为什么重要**：Aldi 90% 以上的商品是自有品牌，「做平替」本来就是它的商业模式；品牌方起诉，是在对抗零售商用它们的设计成果赚钱。
  - Why it matters: over 90% of Aldi's items are private label, so "doing dupes" is its business model; the brand's suit is a pushback against a retailer monetizing the brand's design work.

- **背景数据**：2024 年自有品牌销售增长 3.9%，全国性品牌只增长 1%。自有品牌在吃品牌的份额，这是诉讼的大背景。
  - Context: in 2024 private-label sales grew 3.9% while national brands grew just 1%; private label is taking share, the backdrop to the suit.

- **理论接口（copyconomy）**：这是「平替经济」的一个法律化实例。[[Gielens 2025 -  copyconomy]] 指出，象征价值/设计线索主导的品类最脆弱；算法奖励的是转化而非来源；出路不是消灭平替，而是投资难以被模仿的属性（community、service、ecosystems、trust）。渠道管理的战场从货架位移到搜索排名与发现路径，接 [[平台渠道权力]]。
  - Theory link: a legal instance of the copyconomy. Gielens (2025) notes that symbol/design-led categories are most vulnerable, algorithms reward conversion not provenance, and the way out is not to kill dupes but to invest in hard-to-copy attributes (community, service, ecosystems, trust); the channel battlefield moves from shelf space to search ranking and discovery paths.
