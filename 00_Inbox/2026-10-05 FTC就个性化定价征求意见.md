# FTC 就个性化定价征求公众意见

来源：美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）公告；主席 Andrew Ferguson 周三声明；引用 Consumer Reports 与 Groundwork Collaborative 对 Instacart 的调查。

- **事件**：FTC 就个性化定价（personalized pricing）征求公众意见。该做法指依据消费者个人数据、以及企业推断的消费者愿付价格（willingness to pay）来调整商品价格。
  - The FTC opened a public comment period on personalized pricing: varying prices based on a consumer's personal data and the firm's estimate of their willingness to pay.

- **主席表态**：Andrew Ferguson 称，消费者看到标价时，希望价格与别人相同，而不是零售商依据其个人数据估算出的「你愿意付多少」。
  - Chair Andrew Ferguson: when consumers see a price tag they expect the same price as everyone else, not one a retailer estimates they'd pay based on their personal data.

- **法律边界**：FTC 表示无法完全禁止个性化定价，但未告知消费者「其数据如何被用于定价」的企业，可能违反《FTC 法》第 5 条及其他法律。
  - Legal line: the FTC says it can't fully ban personalized pricing, but firms that don't disclose how consumer data is used to set prices may violate Section 5 of the FTC Act and other laws.

- **Instacart 调查**：Consumer Reports 与 Groundwork Collaborative 发现同一商品在不同顾客间价差最高达 23%。调查后，Instacart 已停止向杂货商提供「可对同一商品同时收取不同价格」的技术。
  - Instacart probe: Consumer Reports and Groundwork found the same item priced up to 23% apart across customers. Since then, Instacart stopped offering grocers the tech that let them charge different prices for the same item simultaneously.

- **民意**：Consumer Reports 2024 年研究显示，三分之二的美国消费者反对这种做法。
  - Sentiment: a 2024 Consumer Reports study found two-thirds of US consumers oppose the practice.

- **一句观点**：Picotte 指出，企业应把个性化定价的短期经济收益，与其对客户忠诚度和信任的长期影响区分开来。
  - One take: Picotte argued firms should separate the short-term economic gains of personalized pricing from its long-term impact on customer loyalty and trust.

- **为何值得记**：是「数据用于定价」监管端的联邦层面动作，与地方透明定价禁令、沃尔玛的公开否认构成同一议题的三个切面。关键不在「禁不禁」，而在「披露义务」与「信任/忠诚」这对长短期权衡，正好接上忠诚度与价格公平的研究线。
  - Worth noting: a federal-level regulatory move on "data-for-pricing," forming one of three facets with the local transparency ban and Walmart's public denial. The crux isn't a ban but the disclosure duty and the short-vs-long-term trust/loyalty tradeoff, which ties into loyalty and price-fairness research.
