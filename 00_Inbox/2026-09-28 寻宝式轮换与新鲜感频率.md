# 寻宝式轮换与「新鲜感频率」（treasure-hunt merchandising / assortment-refresh velocity）

关于折扣店「每周轮换区」的赚钱机制、福祉代价与研究问题。承 [[2026-09-22 Direction]] 里 Newness / refresh frequency 构念，理论母体是 [[反身性零售 reflexive retail]] 的 velocity。

## 为什么轮换赚钱（retailer 视角）

- **店内两块结构**：一块是固定品类（面包、牛奶，永远在），一块是店中间的每周轮换区，卖限量的锅具、衣服、家居用品，卖完不补货。
  - Two zones: a fixed core (bread, milk, always there) and a weekly rotating middle aisle of limited pots, clothes and homeware that is not restocked once sold out.

- **好处一，想再来**：下周货就变了，顾客会为看新货多跑几趟；稀缺感（卖完就没）制造紧迫，冲动消费更多。
  - Makes people return: next week's stock differs, so shoppers visit more often to see what's new; scarcity creates urgency and more impulse buying.

- **好处二，顺路多买**：轮换区放在店中间，你想拿牛奶就必须穿过它（强制动线）。
  - Makes people buy extra: placed mid-store, you must walk through it to reach the milk (a forced path).

- **好处三，零成本试错**：轮换区里哪款卖得好，零售商就知道下次该进什么，无需为新品做长期承诺。
  - Zero-cost test-and-learn: whatever sells tells the retailer what to buy next, without long-term commitment to new items.

- **数据佐证**：这类中间货架占 Aldi 总销售的 10% 到 15%，按利润率算比杂货部分更赚钱，所以英国的 Morrisons 也在模仿。
  - Data: these middle aisles make up 10–15% of Aldi's sales and are more profitable by margin than groceries, which is why the UK's Morrisons is copying it.

- **Action = 整店都是中间货架**：荷兰起家的非食品折扣连锁，3,423 家店，约三分之二的品类定期更换，每周上 150 多款新品。
  - Action (a Dutch non-food discount chain) is like a store that is all middle aisle: 3,423 stores, about two-thirds of the assortment rotates, 150+ new items a week.

## 福祉代价：同一轮换的另一面（consumer 视角，你论文 §6 Spending-Control Gaps）

- **In the paper（§6.2 treasure-hunt merchandising）**：现象覆盖折扣店中间货架、Aldi 轮换 Finds、「Middle of Lidl」、Action、TK Maxx、Costco，线上则是 Temu/Shein 的倒计时和转盘机制。作者认为线上是同一逻辑的放大版：信息流没有尽头，缺少线下「逛完店就停」的自然终点。
  - The paper (§6.2) spans discount middle aisles, Aldi Finds, "Middle of Lidl," Action, TK Maxx, Costco, and online the countdowns and spin-wheels of Temu/Shein; online is the same logic amplified, an endless feed with no natural stopping point like leaving a store.

- **In the paper（核心论点，作者观点）**：寻宝陈列把购买从「需求驱动」推向「机会驱动」。价格低到每件都显得微不足道，人们逐件评估「划算」，而不是看累计花了多少。所以低价零售能提高可负担性（affordability），却不一定让家庭真正省钱。
  - Core claim (authors' view): treasure-hunt display shifts buying from need-driven to opportunity-driven. Prices are so low each item feels trivial, so people judge "a deal" item by item rather than watching cumulative spend. Low-price retail raises affordability but does not necessarily save households money.

- **In the paper（§6.1 smart-shopper positioning）**：引用 Gijsbrechts 等（2018），硬折扣店的顾客并没有因价格低而减少杂货总支出，因为买的量更大、篮子更满；机制是心理账户和自我许可（「我省了钱，可以多买点」）。
  - The paper (§6.1) cites Gijsbrechts et al. (2018): hard-discounter shoppers do not cut total grocery spend despite low prices, because they buy more and fill fuller baskets; the mechanism is mental accounting and self-licensing ("I saved, so I can buy more").

- **In the paper（§9 Table 3 的态度）**：作者并不主张取缔寻宝陈列，因为这会牺牲「发现的乐趣」这类体验价值；立场是「要看在什么条件下」，而非简单说好或坏。
  - The paper (§9, Table 3) does not advocate banning treasure-hunt display, since that would sacrifice experiential value like the joy of discovery; the stance is "it depends on conditions," not simply good or bad.

## 研究问题

- 当折扣零售商加快「新品轮换」的节奏时，到某一点是否反而越来越不划算？（新鲜感疲劳、运营成本、核心形象稀释）
  - Retailer side: as discounters speed up rotation, is there a point where it stops paying off (novelty fatigue, operating cost, dilution of core image)?

- 更快的（自有品牌）轮换对上游快消品牌商意味着什么？
  - Brand side: what does faster (private-label) rotation mean for upstream CPG brands?

- 寻宝的「可负担性」是否真的转化成家庭省钱，还是相反？（承接论文立场）
  - Welfare side: does treasure-hunt affordability actually translate into household savings, or the opposite? (extends the paper's stance)

- **干净的经验场景：Action**。它在你所在地（欧洲），数据可得性与可接触性可能更好；商业模式本身就建立在「新鲜感供给」上，是研究「新鲜感频率」很干净的场景。
  - A clean empirical setting: Action. It is in your region (Europe) with likely better data access, and its business model is built on supplying novelty, a clean setting to study novelty frequency.

## 理论接口

- **velocity**：寻宝轮换是 [[反身性零售 reflexive retail]] 核心变量 velocity（遇见—评估—替换的速度）在实体店里最干净的化身；「机会驱动 + affordability↑ savings↛」就是那篇的折扣悖论。
