# FTC 正式问询九家企业「监控定价」

来源：Grocery Dive，[原文链接](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/ftc-orders-target-walmart-personalized-pricing-inquiry/)

- **事件**：FTC 向九家企业（含沃尔玛、塔吉特）发出正式问询函（formal orders），要求说明是否利用消费者个人数据实施「监控定价（surveillance pricing）」，即对不同顾客就同一商品收取不同价格。企业须在 45 天内回应。
  - The FTC sent formal orders to nine companies, including Walmart and Target, demanding details on whether they vary prices by shopper using personal data, so-called surveillance pricing. Companies have 45 days to respond.

- **企业回应**：塔吉特称其不按顾客身份调价；沃尔玛则为其电子价签（digital price tags）系统进行辩护。
  - Target says it does not price by customer identity; Walmart has defended its digital price-tag system.

- **为何值得记**：这是「数据用于定价」监管从「征求意见、地方立法」升级到「对点名企业发强制性问询」的一步，带硬性时限与披露压力。它把此前的三个切面（FTC 征意见、马里兰州禁令、沃尔玛公开否认）推进到「举证阶段」，关键依旧是披露义务与信任/忠诚的长短期权衡。
  - Worth noting: it escalates "data-for-pricing" oversight from comment periods and local bans to compulsory, named-company inquiries with a hard deadline and disclosure pressure. It pushes the earlier thread into an evidentiary phase, with the disclosure duty and the trust/loyalty tradeoff still at the crux.
