# 渠道权力从货架转向软件层：Instacart 与 DoorDash

来源：本周第二部分两则新闻合读（Instacart 终止 Caper Cart；Instacart 被 DoorDash 收购）。

- **事件一**：Instacart 终止智能购物车「Caper Cart」业务，原因是零售商采用速度太慢，公司转而明确押注「智能与软件（intelligence and software）」。Caper Cart 本是把屏幕与推荐引擎直接装进购物车、嵌入货架旁边的尝试。
  - Instacart killed its Caper Cart smart-cart hardware, a literal attempt to put a screen and recommendation engine onto the shelf-adjacent cart, because retailers didn't adopt it fast enough, pivoting explicitly to "intelligence and software."

- **事件二**：两天后，Instacart 同意被 DoorDash 收购。DoorDash 的核心价值在于配送 App 的排序与推荐算法，而非任何实体门店资产。
  - Two days later, Instacart agreed to be bought by DoorDash, whose entire value proposition is the delivery app's ranking and recommendation logic, not any physical store asset.

- **合读的含义（确认性案例）**：影响顾客购买决策的发力点，正从店内陈列转向决定顾客在线上「看到什么」的软件层，是 [[平台渠道权力]] 框架一次相当干净的印证。
  - Read together, a clean confirming example: the leverage point for what a shopper buys is moving from in-store merchandising to the software layer that decides what a shopper even sees online.

- **一个补丁**：Caper Cart 的失败说明零售商对「哪些平台技术能进入自己门店」仍握有真实的否决权（veto power），渠道权力并非单向流向平台。
  - A wrinkle: Caper Cart's failure shows retailers still have real veto power over which platform technologies enter their stores; channel power isn't flowing one-directionally to platforms.

- **开放问题**：DoorDash 与 Instacart 合并后的规模，是否会削弱零售商未来的这种否决权？即平台整合是否会把一种「可谈判」的合作关系，变成类似零售商过去对供应商所握有的强势「货架权力（shelf power）」？
  - Open question: does the combined DoorDash-Instacart scale erode retailers' veto power going forward, turning a negotiable relationship into something closer to the shelf power retailers once held over suppliers?
