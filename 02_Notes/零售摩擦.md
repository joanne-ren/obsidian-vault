# 零售摩擦（retail friction）

- 指购物者在购物全流程中遇到的各种阻力：找货、排队结账、退货、联系客服等。
  - The various frictions shoppers encounter across the shopping journey: finding items, checkout queues, returns, contacting support.
- 技术投入常常降低了**零售商**的运营摩擦，却把摩擦**转移**给了购物者，而非真正消除。
  - Tech investment often lowers the retailer's operational friction but shifts it onto shoppers rather than eliminating it.
- 与 [[购物者体验]] 直接相关：摩擦是影响体验的核心变量之一。
